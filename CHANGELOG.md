# Changelog

## v1.0.0 (2026-11-25)

The first public release, under its new name, Trafika (formerly "Fixer").

### Apps
- A Linux app as an AppImage, which runs on most distros.
- A web app on GitHub Pages: nothing to install, works offline after the first visit.
- Both apps are built from the same code.

### Planning
- **Split view:** the Instagram post (with profile grid) next to the zine (flip, sheet and fold views).
- **Photos:**
  - Single, pair and stacked slides.
  - Separate or linked crops for the post and the print.
  - Separate or linked borders for each side.
- **Covers:**
  - 12 title layouts.
  - Any font, Latin and Cyrillic.
  - Layout grids with snapping.
  - Wraparound covers.
- **Back page:**
  - A note, place, song and your own fields.
  - A movable, recolourable barcode and QR code.
- **Templates:** T1 8-page mini, T2 mini + poster, T4 stapled booklet (8–24 pages), T6 French fold, T7 accordion with glue tab, T8 tri-fold.

### Output
- **Instagram exports:** 1:1, 4:5, 3:4 and 1.91:1 JPEGs, with no camera or GPS data.
- **Print PDFs:** 300 DPI, with guides, per-copy serials and an EAN-13 + 2 barcode for every copy.
- **Collection ledger:** a print log and barcode search.

### Data
- `.zip` backups, for one issue or the whole collection, that open in both apps.

### Privacy
- No network requests, no uploads, no accounts, nothing posted for you.
