# Trafika user guide

## Your first issue in 10 steps

1. **New issue.** Click **New issue** at the top right. One issue is one Instagram post and one printed zine.
2. **Add photos.** Drop them on the window, or click **Add photos**. JPEG, PNG and WebP all work. A landscape photo becomes a *pair*: two slides on Instagram, and one spread across two pages in the zine.
3. **Order them.** Drag the slides in the left column. The zine pages follow the carousel order.
4. **Crop (CRP).** Drag the photo inside its frame, or scroll to zoom. The post and the print can share one crop or have their own.
5. **Borders (BRD).** Pick a border for each side, for example full-bleed on Instagram and a paper mat in print. Click **Link borders** to keep them the same.
6. **Cover (CVR).** Type the title and subtitle, choose fonts (Latin and Cyrillic) and pick a title layout. Turn the grid on with `G`; things snap to it as you drag.
7. **Back page (BCK).** Write the note, place and song, and drag the barcode and QR code where you want them.
8. **Template (TPL).** Choose how the zine is made: the 8-page mini (T1), a stapled booklet (T4), an accordion (T7)… Use **Flip** to read it, **Sheet** to see the print, and **Fold** for the steps.
9. **Serials and caption (SER).** Set how many copies are in this edition and write the Instagram caption.
10. **Export.** **Export IG slides** gives you the JPEGs and the caption, ready to post yourself. **Make print PDF** makes the print file. Every copy gets its own serial and barcode, and the run is logged in the ledger.

Shortcuts: `V` move, `H` hand (or hold Space), `T` text, `G` grid, `Ctrl` + scroll to zoom, `Ctrl+0` to reset the zoom, `Ctrl+Z` / `Ctrl+Shift+Z` to undo and redo, arrow keys to nudge (hold `Shift` for bigger steps).

## Backups

- **Linux app:** your issues are plain folders in `~/Zines`.
- **Web app:** they live in your browser's storage, which the browser can clear. Use **Collection → Back up collection (.zip)** now and then; the status bar reminds you after 14 days.
- **Restoring:** **Import backup…** brings a `.zip` back, in either app.

## Printer calibration

Every printer has a strip at each edge it can't print on, and double-sided printing rarely lines up perfectly.

1. Click **Printer → Make calibration page**.
2. Print it at **actual size**: 100 %, with "fit to page" off. For double-sided templates, print it on both sides, flipping on the short edge.
3. Read the margins off the rulers and type them in. Hold side A against the light and read how far side B is shifted, then type that in as the duplex offset.
4. The margins now show as red hatching in the **Sheet** view. Keep anything important out of them.

Prints usually come out darker than the screen. **Print brightness lift** lightens the photos in print files only; Instagram exports are never changed.

## Trimming and folding

- **Trimmed sheet (3:4 pages).** With **Trim before folding** on, the print has small marks at the top and bottom. Cut along them first, with a craft knife and a metal ruler, so the sheet is 297 × 198 mm. Every page then comes out exactly 3:4.
- **Test first.** **Numbered test print** prints the sheet with big page numbers. Fold it and check the numbers run 1 to 8 before printing photos.
- **Folds.** Crease every fold hard with a bone folder or the back of a spoon. 100–120 gsm paper folds cleanly; thicker paper cracks at the folds.
- **Steps.** **Fold** in the zine view shows each template step by step. Red lines are cuts, dashed lines are folds. On the accordion (T7), glue or double-sided tape on the tab joins the two strips.
- **Stapled booklet (T4).** Nest the sheets in order with sheet 1 outside, then staple twice through the fold. A long-arm stapler makes this easier.
